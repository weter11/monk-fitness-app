    as the one place a session captures its configuration, `WorkoutSessionUiState` presenting it,
    and `ActiveWorkoutConfiguration` holding it with no persistence. Every one of those subjects
    is retired, so all eight of its claims are false by construction rather than merely
    inconvenient. The invariant it protected is the target session snapshot's, captured at
    `SessionRuntime.startSession` and never re-derived — pinned by the session runtime's suites
    and by `WorkoutSession`'s own construction rules.
